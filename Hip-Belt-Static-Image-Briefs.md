# SECTION B: FORMAT VARIATIONS

Take each headline concept above and adapt to these 5 formats. Below are the format templates.

---

## Format 1: Original (Reference Ad)

**Layout:** Top bar text > Hero image (model wearing belt, belt glowing) > Big stat headline overlaid > Bullet points beside product > CTA bar at bottom

**Image:** Woman (40-60), casual clothing, warm home background, smiling, active posture. Belt on hip, red lights visible.

**Text placement:** Headline large and bold over/beside the image. Bullets in white. CTA bar dark with bold white text.

**Use this for:** All 7 headline concepts as the base version.

---

## Format 2: Product Hero

**Layout:** Product only -- no model. Belt centred on clean background. Headline above. Features/benefits below. CTA at bottom.

**Image:** Belt photographed flat or floating, angled to show the red light panels. Dark or gradient background. No person.

**Text placement:** Headline top-centre. Product mid-frame. Call-outs or bullets below product pointing to features. CTA bar at bottom.

**Instruction:** Strip the model. Let the product sell itself. Works best with Ad 1 (12 months), Ad 7 (20 minutes), and Ad 2 (first try). Add "Engineered in Sydney" badge near product.

**Adapt each headline by:**
- Keeping headline and CTA identical
- Replacing model image with product-only hero shot
- Adding feature arrows/labels pointing to belt components (red light panel, heat element, vibration motor, infrared array)

---

## Format 3: Old Man

**Layout:** Same as Format 1 but swap the model.

**Image:** Man (55-70), wearing the belt. Home setting or outdoor (garden, workshop, golf course). Active but relatable -- not athletic. Belt on hip, red glow visible.

**Instruction:** Same text as Format 1. Only change is the model. Targets male audience 50+ who are facing the surgery decision. Posture should say "I can still do things" -- not clinical, not defeated.

**Adapt each headline by:**
- Keeping all text identical to the original format
- Swapping woman for older man
- Adjusting setting to male-skewing activity (workshop, garden, walking the dog, golf)

# NOTES FOR DESIGNER

- All headlines: surgery angle leads. Product is the answer, not the opener.
- Keep the red glow visible on all belt shots -- it's the visual proof of "this does something."
- Yellow/gold for key stats and numbers. White for body text. Red for warning/surgery stats.
- Dark backgrounds perform better for this product category.
- Every ad must have the money-back guarantee visible. It's the risk-reversal that makes the surgery comparison work.
- "Engineered in Sydney" badge on every version -- local trust signal for AU audience.
- Testimonials are illustrative examples for the brief. Replace with real customer testimonials before production.
