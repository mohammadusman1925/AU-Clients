## HIP-NEW-16 — LONGFORM

Name: Hip Brace - The Drawer of Things That Failed
Version: V1

Desire: Stop wasting hope on nothing
Avatar: Burned-out pain veteran
Persona: Tired believer who stopped believing
Concept: The drawer of failed attempts
Angle: Everyone with chronic hip pain has a drawer full of things they bought that did nothing, and opening it feels like looking at a graveyard of hope.
Core Message: This is the first thing that didn't end up in that drawer.

Level of Awareness: Solution Aware
Funnel: TOFU

Hook 1: Everyone with hip pain has this drawer. You know the one.
Hook 2: I stopped counting how much money is sitting in that drawer doing nothing.
Hook 3: My doctor never told me about the drawer. But she's got one too.

Body:
You know the drawer. The one you don't open anymore. The tube of something that smelled like a pharmacy. The wrap that lost its stretch after a week. The thing you saw at 2am on your phone and thought maybe, maybe this one. You've got receipts in there you don't want to look at. And every time you bought something new, there was this little voice that said this is going to work. And it never did. So you stopped listening to that voice. You stopped trying. You just started living with it. Adjusting. Moving slower. Saying no to things you used to love without even realizing you were doing it. I found this brace because someone I trust told me about it. Not an ad. Not a review. A person who walks the way I used to walk. And now she doesn't. I wore it for three days before I even told anyone. Because I didn't want to hear myself say it's working and then have to take that back. But it's been weeks now. And the drawer is still closed. And I don't think about it anymore.

CTA:
Try it for yourself. If it ends up in the drawer, send it back. 365 days to decide.
