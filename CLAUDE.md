# AU-Clients Project Memory

## Creative Framework Definitions

### Use Case
A Use Case is the specific situation your customer is in. Not a demographic. Not an age bracket. Not "women 25-45." It's a real-life scene with a real person in a real moment.

Use Case = your ICP in a situation.

Examples:
- Concert goer at a festival
- Musician in rehearsal
- Office worker in a loud space
- Traveller at the airport
- Woman at the mirror, 7am, skin not improving despite doing everything right

They all put a person inside a moment. You can see them. You can feel the tension. That's the point. The Use Case feeds into a formula for ad creative iteration.
