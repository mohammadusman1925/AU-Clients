Cortisone & Your Kidneys

What Cortisone Specifically Does to Your Kidneys

Cortisone doesn't just fail to fix your shoulder. It actively stresses the organs you need to stay alive.

  • Fluid retention and increased blood pressure — both directly stress the kidneys. Elevated blood pressure is the #2 cause of kidney disease. StatCare
  • Blood sugar spikes — 78% glucose increase on day 1 (215% for Type 1 diabetics). Sustained high blood sugar damages kidney blood vessels. Healio / AAFP
  • Nephrocalcinosis and kidney stones — documented renal complications from corticosteroid use. Joint Rehab
  • Risk elevated for patients with pre-existing CKD, diabetes, or heart conditions — exactly the populations most likely to have shoulder problems. MedXDRG
