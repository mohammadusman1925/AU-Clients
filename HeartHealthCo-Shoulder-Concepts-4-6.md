## SHOULDER ANGLE

### 04   The pain improved, but movement did not

> **CORE MESSAGE**
> *"Less pain does not always mean normal movement has returned."*

> **PAIN POINT**
> The constant ache decreases, so the person assumes recovery. But reaching behind the back, lifting the arm, and doing the hair remain difficult. Confusion builds about whether the condition is actually improving.

**Story**
The constant ache begins to decrease, so the person assumes the shoulder is recovering. However, reaching behind the back, lifting the arm and doing the hair remain difficult. This creates confusion about whether the condition is improving. Present Heart Health as a consistent comfort and mobility-support routine, with progress measured through everyday movements rather than pain alone.

**Why it may work**
This speaks to people who are partially improved but still frustrated. They are more solution-aware and may be ready to try something new because their original pain solution did not restore function.

**Best formats**
*Range-of-motion diary, educational animation and customer story.*

## SHOULDER ANGLE

### 05   Life becomes smaller one activity at a time

> **CORE MESSAGE**
> *"Pain rarely takes everything at once. It takes one normal thing at a time."*

> **PAIN POINT**
> First, sleeping on one side stops. Then carrying groceries, preparing food, driving, showering, and hobbies become harder. Eventually, daily life revolves around avoiding certain movements. Independence shrinks gradually.

**Story**
First, the person stops sleeping on one side. Then carrying groceries, preparing food, driving, showering and hobbies become harder. Eventually, daily life revolves around avoiding certain movements. The story shifts the endpoint from "reduce pain" to "protect the life and independence you still have."

**Why it may work**
Loss aversion is more powerful than a generic relief promise. The public Facebook shoulder group repeatedly frames the problem as life becoming smaller and normal activities no longer feeling easy.

**Best formats**
*Emotional long-form ad and day-in-the-life UGC.*

## SHOULDER ANGLE

### 06   When it is your dominant arm and you live alone

> **CORE MESSAGE**
> *"When the painful shoulder is the arm you rely on for everything."*

> **PAIN POINT**
> Every daily task requires a workaround - front-clasp bra, spray deodorant, pre-cut vegetables, a different computer mouse. Each workaround costs time, money, or independence. No one is there to help.

**Story**
The person switches to a front-clasp bra, spray deodorant, pre-cut vegetables and a different computer mouse. Every workaround costs time, money or independence. Demonstrate how a hands-free wearable routine can fit into the day without requiring someone else to hold a device or repeatedly apply creams.

**Why it may work**
Independence is a powerful emotional endpoint. The product's wearable design is particularly relevant to people who live alone or cannot rely on daily assistance. These exact workarounds appear repeatedly in frozen-shoulder discussions.

**Best formats**
*Day-in-the-life UGC showing five frustrating workarounds.*
